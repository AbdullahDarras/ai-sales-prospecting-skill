# Limitations (read before promising results)

Measured on real runs (Gulf, clinics, restaurants, service companies):
- **No lead passed the automatic thresholds** (verification 75 and fit 70). Public data rarely proves the owner,
  maps status, or last social post. Expect most good leads to land in `undecided` for human review.
- Google Maps listings and logged-out Instagram pages are generally not readable by agents. Maps status stays
  `unknown` (costs 10 of 30 existence points); social freshness is often unknown.
- Owners of cafes and restaurants are rarely public, so decision-maker points are usually 0.
- Multi-branch businesses show several phone numbers; the layer flags this as a conflict even when it is normal.
- Company-size numbers come from directories and LinkedIn snippets and can disagree.
- Thresholds are provisional. Calibrate with 5-10 of your best past customers before trusting them.
- Discovery quality varies by city; some results belong to another country (the phone-format check catches many).
- Message rules and the dialect filter are Arabic-specific.
- Legal: respect each platform's terms, local data-protection law (e.g. KSA, UAE, Jordan) and always honour opt-outs.
  This tool does not send anything; that responsibility stays with the human.
